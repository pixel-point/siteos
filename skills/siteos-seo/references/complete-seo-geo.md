# Complete SEO/GEO

Use this workflow for a full SEO/GEO setup, review or improvement request. Cover every applicable
area below, choose useful measurements for this website and finish the authorized work. A focused
request stays focused. A complete review does not mean executing every available check, choosing
maximum limits or making every result green.

## Establish the project and current coverage

Follow [Project research](project-research.md) to establish the exact Project/environment, product,
audience, customer needs, conversion paths, market and important pages. Reuse context already
established in this conversation. Verify a matching repository before using its contents or changing
it; continue from the public website and saved reports when no matching checkout is available.

For requested setup, connect SEO when absent using [Technical audit](technical-audit.md). This only
creates the selected environment's resource. Inspect current audit status, saved Performance and
research reports, GSC binding/freshness, saved research inputs and existing audit schedules. Reuse
appropriate observations and settings. Do not reset an existing configuration or duplicate inputs
merely to make the setup look new.

Keep a compact coverage ledger: area, available evidence/date/scope, next useful action and status.
Use it to continue across stages and to distinguish checked, partial, awaiting access or approval,
and not applicable with a reason. Missing GSC, an unavailable platform, exhausted research credits
or a pending crawl does not block independent work in other areas.

Analytics referrals and conversions can inform priorities when already available through
`$siteos-analytics`; do not turn their absence into a requirement to install another service.

separate observations. Use text-based Mentions as the primary category metric with its own readable-answer denominator and ambiguous-name exclusions. AEO and First choice need valid supporting evidence and a disclosed
denominator; report them as unavailable when absent. Do not discard useful textual evidence merely
because it cannot supply an AEO score, or present a manual observation as a saved calculated metric.

## Prepare and execute a bounded measurement plan

For missing research, derive exact inputs from project evidence before calling `research plan`.
Deduplicate questions and avoid measuring the same need through multiple paid workflows without a
reason. Review every area, but explain when an existing report or a more useful check covers its
question. Save useful reusable inputs when setup is requested, preserving unrelated saved checks.

Consolidate proposed paid work into one reviewable scope: purposes, exact keywords/questions,
products/domains, market/language, devices, platforms, planned parts and the available estimate or
credit information. An account balance or admission reservation is not an exact price estimate.
For a complete AI comparison, propose every platform supported by the current release and identical
questions on each, unless the user's narrower scope or budget says otherwise. Use current help
and validated plans, not hard-coded provider lists or maximum-size batches.

Execute checks already covered by the user's scope and budget without asking again. A broad full
review alone does not authorize unlimited paid research: when scope or spending remains unspecified,
ask once about the concrete plan while continuing saved-evidence analysis, applicable non-credit
manual checks and authorized local fixes. The entire plan must fit the shared balance/budget;
validating each request individually does not establish that the combined batch fits.

Use [research operations](search-research.md) for idempotent launch, bounded waiting, partial
reports and export. Read back admitted work to its terminal state when possible. Retain failed
observations; do not automatically spend again to repair formatting or complete every platform.
If a stage needs user access, leave a concrete pending step and continue independent stages.
New recurring schedules, notification recipients, outreach and publication require their own
applicable authorization; existing authorization counts.

## Deliver fixes and verified outcomes

For an improvement/setup request, implement coherent evidence-backed fixes in the matching
repository when available. For a review-only request, prepare the changes instead. Distinguish
technical fixes from content proposals that need product facts or editorial decisions. Run the
repository's applicable checks and preserve its architecture, framework conventions and user work.

Respect the publication scope. A passing local check is not a resolved finding on the public site:
after an authorized deployment, use completed rechecks for affected observed URLs. Track local
fixes awaiting deployment or recheck separately. Visibility changes require later comparable
observations and never establish causation or a guaranteed ranking gain by themselves.

Finish with a concise coverage ledger and a prioritized page/query action list backed by report IDs,
dates, source URLs and relevant files. State what was configured, measured, changed and verified;
include research spend when reported, deferred work and the next concrete action for each blocker.
Propose recurring audits or notifications when they serve the website, with scope and cadence ready
for approval if not already authorized. Do not call the whole project complete while an applicable
area has silently been omitted or a queued job has been presented as a completed check.
